# sn46.39: SN 46.39 Rukkhasutta — Trees

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Both lists (2.1, 2.3-2.7; 3.1, 3.3); the hindrances, parasites of the mind, weaken wisdom (2.1, 2.8; stated of each hindrance in the Pali only, 2.3-2.7); developing the awakening factors leads to the realization of the fruit of knowledge and freedom (3.1, 3.3-3.4).

What we say we left out: The simile of the large parasitic fig trees whose host trees collapse (1.1-1.4). Its point is a renunciate overcome by sensual pleasures like those he gave up, or worse (1.5), which is not modelled, so under D22 the simile stays out too. That the hindrances are obstacles and the awakening factors are not (2.1, 3.1): no pattern yet for what a quality is or is not (as SN 46.37).
