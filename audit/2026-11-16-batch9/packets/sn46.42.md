### c-u3 (condition, layer: sutta)
- summary: the appearance of a Realized One when present, then the seven awakening factors: arise (“When a Realized One, a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha appears the seven treasures of the awakening factors appear”)
- condition: the appearance of a Realized One
- relation: when present, then
- conditioned: the seven awakening factors
- result: arise
- wording: When a Realized One, a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha appears the seven treasures of the awakening factors appear
- note: SN 46.42 calls the factors 'treasures' (bojjhaṅgaratana) and says they appear with (pātubhāvā) a Realized One's appearance; SN 46.9 (c-u1) says they don't arise apart from it. Two claims, kept apart.
- segments cited in this text: sn46.42:2.1, sn46.42:2.4

### sim-monarch (simile, layer: sutta)
- summary: simile: the seven treasures of a Wheel-Turning Monarch, illustrating the appearance of a Realized One, the seven awakening factors
- wording: When a Realized One, a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha appears the seven treasures of the awakening factors appear.
- note: The text sets the two side by side without 'just as … in the same way' (seyyathāpi … evameva); treated as a simile because the monarch's treasures are there only to illustrate (D23). The seven treasures are named in 1.3.
- segments cited in this text: sn46.42:1.1, sn46.42:1.3, sn46.42:1.4, sn46.42:2.1
