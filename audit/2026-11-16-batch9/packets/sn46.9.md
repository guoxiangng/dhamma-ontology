### c-u1 (condition, layer: sutta)
- summary: the appearance of a Realized One is required for the seven awakening factors: arise (“don’t arise to be developed and cultivated except when a Realized One, a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha has appeared”)
- condition: the appearance of a Realized One
- relation: is required for
- conditioned: the seven awakening factors
- result: arise
- wording: don’t arise to be developed and cultivated except when a Realized One, a perfected one, a fully awakened Buddha has appeared
- note: Pali: 'these seven awakening factors, developed and cultivated, arise unarisen, not apart from (nāññatra) the appearance of a Realized One'. 'To be developed and cultivated' is Sujato's reading of bhāvitā bahulīkatā. A necessary condition, not a sufficient one (D23).
