# sn46.33: SN 46.33 Upakkilesasutta — Corruptions

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The five corruptions of the mind as a list whose members are the same five qualities as the hindrances (2.1, 2.3, 2.4). The simile of the five corruptions of native gold (1.1-1.8, 2.1; D22).

What we say we left out: What the corruptions do: the mind is not pliable, workable or radiant, is brittle, and is not rightly immersed for the ending of defilements (2.1, 2.4). No pattern yet for similes or for what a quality does.

## Claims citing this text

### FiveCorruptionsOfMind (list, layer: sutta)
- summary: the five corruptions of the mind (5): doubt, dullness and drowsiness, ill will, restlessness and remorse, sensual desire
- members: ['doubt', 'dullness and drowsiness', 'ill will', 'restlessness and remorse', 'sensual desire']
- expectedCount: 5
- note: In SN 46.33:2.3 the Pali names only sensual desire and abbreviates the rest (…pe…); the other four members are the conventional expansion, as the translation gives them.
- segments cited in this text: sn46.33:2.1, sn46.33:2.3, sn46.33:2.4

### sim-gold (simile, layer: sutta)
- summary: simile: the five corruptions of native gold, illustrating the five corruptions of the mind
- wording: In the same way, there are these five corruptions of the mind. When the mind is corrupted by these it’s not pliable, workable, or radiant. It’s brittle, and not rightly immersed in samādhi for the ending of defilements.
- note: Iron, copper, tin, lead and silver corrupt gold as the five hindrances corrupt the mind (1.3-1.7).
- segments cited in this text: sn46.33:1.1, sn46.33:1.3, sn46.33:1.7, sn46.33:1.8, sn46.33:2.1
