# sn46.1: SN 46.1 Himavantasutta — The Himalaya

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The seven factors developed depending on and grounded on ethics (1.4), and the stock formula for each factor (relies on seclusion, fading away and cessation; ripens as letting go) (1.6, 1.12). The simile of dragons growing strong on the Himalayas (1.2-1.4; D22).

What we say we left out: The result 'acquiring great and abundant good qualities' (1.4, 1.13): kept in the simile's point (D22), not modelled as an outcome. The simile itself is encoded (1.2-1.4).

### sim-dragons (simile, layer: sutta)
- summary: simile: dragons growing strong on the Himalayas, illustrating ethics, the seven awakening factors
- wording: In the same way, a mendicant develops and cultivates the seven awakening factors depending on and grounded on ethics, acquiring great and abundant good qualities.
- note: The dragons grow strong on the mountain, then go down to pools, lakes, streams, rivers and the ocean, acquiring a great and abundant body (1.3); so the mendicant, grounded on ethics, acquires great and abundant good qualities.
- segments cited in this text: sn46.1:1.2, sn46.1:1.3, sn46.1:1.4
