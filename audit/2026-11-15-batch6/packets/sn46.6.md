# sn46.6: SN 46.6 Kuṇḍaliyasutta — Kuṇḍaliya

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The fulfilment ladder (D20): sense restraint fulfils the three kinds of good conduct, which fulfil the four kinds of mindfulness meditation, which fulfil the seven awakening factors, which fulfil knowledge and freedom (2.1-2.8, with the closing line of each section: 5.7, 6.3, 7.6, 8.4). The three kinds of good conduct (6.2) and the four kinds of mindfulness meditation (7.2-7.5) as lists. The stock formula (8.2-8.3).

What we say we left out: Kuṇḍaliya's framing (1.4-1.8). 'The benefit the Realized One lives for is the fruit of knowledge and freedom' (1.9): no pattern for what someone lives for. How each step is developed: sense restraint toward agreeable and disagreeable sights and the other senses (3.2-5.6), giving up bad conduct to develop good conduct (6.2), the observing practice of each mindfulness meditation (7.2-7.5): described, not modelled beyond the lists. Kuṇḍaliya's going for refuge (9.1-9.5).
