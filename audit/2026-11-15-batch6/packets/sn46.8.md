# sn46.8: SN 46.8 Upavānasutta — With Upavāṇa

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Taught by Venerable Upavāṇa to Sāriputta: the seven awakening factors, properly implemented by rational application of mind, lead to living at ease (2.1-2.2, 3.3).

What we say we left out: The setting (1.1-1.3). What a mendicant understands as they rouse up each factor: 'my mind is well freed, dullness and drowsiness eradicated, restlessness and remorse eliminated, energy roused up, mind sharply focused, not sluggish' (3.1-3.2). No pattern yet for what is known in practice; read as outcomes it would overstate the text.

## Claims citing this text

### o-33 (outcome, layer: sutta)
- summary: the seven awakening factors leads to great ease (“the seven awakening factors have been properly implemented so that they lead to living at ease”)
- of: the seven awakening factors
- effect: leads to
- to: great ease
- wording: the seven awakening factors have been properly implemented so that they lead to living at ease
- note: Taught by Venerable Upavāṇa in answer to Sāriputta. 'Properly implemented' (susamāraddhā) 'by rational application of mind' (yonisomanasikārā); Sujato renders the last 'by investigating inside themselves'.
- segments cited in this text: sn46.8:2.1, sn46.8:2.2, sn46.8:3.3

sn46.8:1.1 | At one time the venerables Upavāṇa and Sāriputta were staying near Kosambī in Ghosita’s monastery. | Ekaṁ samayaṁ āyasmā ca upavāno āyasmā ca sāriputto kosambiyaṁ viharanti ghositārāme.
sn46.8:1.2 | Then in the late afternoon, Venerable Sāriputta came out of retreat, went to Venerable Upavāṇa and exchanged greetings with him. | Atha kho āyasmā sāriputto sāyanhasamayaṁ paṭisallānā vuṭṭhito yenāyasmā upavāno tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā āyasmatā upavānena saddhiṁ sammodi.
sn46.8:1.3 | When the greetings and polite conversation were over, he sat down to one side and said to Upavāṇa: | Sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sāraṇīyaṁ vītisāretvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā sāriputto āyasmantaṁ upavānaṁ etadavoca:
sn46.8:2.1 | “Reverend Upavāṇa, can a mendicant know that by investigating inside themselves, the seven awakening factors have been properly implemented so that they lead to living at ease?” | “Jāneyya nu kho, āvuso upavāna, bhikkhu ‘paccattaṁ yonisomanasikārā evaṁ susamāraddhā me satta bojjhaṅgā phāsuvihārāya saṁvattantī’”ti?
sn46.8:2.2 | “They can, Reverend Sāriputta. | “Jāneyya kho, āvuso sāriputta, bhikkhu ‘paccattaṁ yonisomanasikārā evaṁ susamāraddhā me satta bojjhaṅgā phāsuvihārāya saṁvattantī’”ti.
sn46.8:3.1 | As a mendicant rouses up the awakening factor of mindfulness, they understand: ‘My mind is well freed. I’ve eradicated dullness and drowsiness, and eliminated restlessness and remorse. sn46.8:3.2 | As they rouse up the awakening factor of equanimity, they understand: ‘My mind is well freed. I’ve eradicated dullness and drowsiness, and eliminated restlessness and remorse.
