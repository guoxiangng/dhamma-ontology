# sn46.5: SN 46.5 Bhikkhusutta — A Monk

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Why they are called awakening factors: they lead to awakening (bodhi) (1.5, 1.11); the list (1.6); the stock formula for each factor (relies on seclusion, fading away and cessation; ripens as letting go) (1.7).

What we say we left out: As they are developed the mind is freed from the defilements of sensuality, desire to be reborn and ignorance, and knows it is freed (1.8-1.10). Needs the three defilements (āsava) as a list and a pattern for release.
