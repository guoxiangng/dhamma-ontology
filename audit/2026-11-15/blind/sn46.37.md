# sn46.37: SN 46.37 Āvaraṇanīvaraṇasutta — Obstacles

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Both lists (1.1, 1.3-1.7; 2.1, 2.3); the hindrances weaken wisdom (1.1, 1.8); developing the awakening factors leads to the realization of the fruit of knowledge and freedom (2.1, 2.4).

What we say we left out: That the hindrances are obstacles and corruptions of the heart, and the awakening factors are not (1.1, 1.8, 2.1, 2.4). Needs a pattern for what a quality is or is not.
