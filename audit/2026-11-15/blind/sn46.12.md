# sn46.12: SN 46.12 Paṭhamasūriyūpamasutta — The Simile of the Sun (1st)

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Good friendship is the forerunner of the arising of the seven factors (1.2), and one with good friends can expect to develop them (1.3); the stock formula for each factor (relies on seclusion, fading away and cessation; ripens as letting go) (2.3).

What we say we left out: The simile of the dawn as forerunner of the sunrise (1.1).
