# sn46.40: SN 46.40 Nīvaraṇasutta — Hindrances

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Both lists (1.1, 1.3; 2.1, 2.3); the hindrances block wisdom and don't lead to extinguishment (1.1, 1.8); the awakening factors grow wisdom and lead to extinguishment (2.1, 2.4).

What we say we left out: The Pali compounds 'making blind, sightless, ignorant' and 'on the side of distress' for the hindrances, and their opposites for the awakening factors (1.1, 2.1), which don't map cleanly onto a target concept; and the chapter's summary verse (3.0-3.4).
