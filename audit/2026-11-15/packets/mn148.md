# mn148: MN 148 Chachakkasutta — Six By Six

Coverage status we recorded: **cited only**

What we say we encoded: Two segments (5.3, 6.8) as evidence for 'dhamma' meaning an object of the mind.

What we say we left out: The six sets of six (sense fields, objects, consciousnesses, contacts, feelings, cravings) and the not-self argument built on them.

## Claims citing this text

### sense-dhamma-mind-object (word sense, layer: lexicon)
- summary: 'dhamma' meaning a dhamma (an object of the mind, an idea)
- segments cited in this text: mn148:5.3, mn148:6.8
