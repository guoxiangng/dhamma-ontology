# sn46.52: SN 46.52 Pariyāyasutta — Is There a Way?

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The way in which the five hindrances become ten (9.1-9.12) and the seven awakening factors become fourteen (10.1-16.3): each of the twelve becomes twofold, as twelve divisions (D18) on four bases: internal and external (sensual desire, ill will, doubt, mindfulness, investigation, equanimity), physical and mental (energy, tranquility), with and without placing the mind (rapture, immersion), and the two qualities a compound name joins (dullness / drowsiness, restlessness / remorse).

What we say we left out: The narrative frame and the wanderers' challenge (1.1-7.2), including their restatement of the teaching as 'give up the five hindrances, corruptions of the heart that weaken wisdom' (3.2, 3.4, 6.1, 6.3): said by the interlocutors (D17), and the characterisation needs a pattern. The Buddha's counter-question to put to them, and that only the Realized One, his disciple or someone who heard it from them could answer it (8.1-8.6): no pattern for claims about who can answer.
