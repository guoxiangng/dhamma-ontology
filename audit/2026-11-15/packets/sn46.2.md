# sn46.2: SN 46.2 Kāyasutta — The Body

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Its 12 statements of what fuels the five hindrances and seven awakening factors (2-6, 9-15). They match SN 46.51 sections 1-12 (identical English; Pali differs only in punctuation and one spelling variant), so they were added as a second citation on the same claims, not as new claims.

What we say we left out: The simile framing the teaching: as the body is sustained by food, so the hindrances and the awakening factors are sustained by fuel (1.2-1.3, 7, 8, 16). There is no pattern for similes yet.
