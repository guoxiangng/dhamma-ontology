# sn46.75: SN 46.75 Virāgasutta — Dispassion

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The one line given: the perception of dispassion (Pali: virāgasaññā), developed and cultivated, is very fruitful and beneficial (1.1).
