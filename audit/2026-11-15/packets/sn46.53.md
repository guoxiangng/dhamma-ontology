# sn46.53: SN 46.53 Aggisutta — Fire

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The timing advice: which awakening factors it is the right or wrong time to develop when the mind is sluggish (4.1, 7.1) or restless (10.1, 13.1), the reason given for each, and 'mindfulness is always useful' (15.4).

What we say we left out: The setting and the challenge from wanderers of other religions (1.1-3.1); the four fire similes (5, 8, 11, 14) that illustrate the advice; the repeated statements after each simile (6, 9, 12, 15.1-15.3).
