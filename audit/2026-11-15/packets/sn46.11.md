# sn46.11: SN 46.11 Pāṇasutta — Living Creatures

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The seven factors developed depending on and grounded on ethics (1.3); the stock formula for each factor (relies on seclusion, fading away and cessation; ripens as letting go) (2.3).

What we say we left out: The simile of creatures in the four postures depending on the earth (1.1-1.2).

sn46.11:1.1 | “Mendicants, whatever living creatures engage in the four postures— | “Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, ye keci pāṇā cattāro iriyāpathe kappenti—
sn46.11:1.2 | sometimes walking, sometimes standing, sometimes sitting, sometimes lying down—all do so depending on the earth and grounded on the earth. | kālena gamanaṁ, kālena ṭhānaṁ, kālena nisajjaṁ, kālena seyyaṁ, sabbe te pathaviṁ nissāya pathaviyaṁ patiṭṭhāya evamete cattāro iriyāpathe kappenti;
sn46.11:1.3 | In the same way, a mendicant develops and cultivates the seven awakening factors depending on and grounded on ethics. | evameva kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sīlaṁ nissāya sīle patiṭṭhāya satta bojjhaṅge bhāveti, satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkaroti.
sn46.11:2.1 | And how does a mendicant develop the seven awakening factors depending on and grounded on ethics? | Kathañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sīlaṁ nissāya sīle patiṭṭhāya satta bojjhaṅge bhāveti satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkaroti?
sn46.11:2.2 | It’s when a mendicant develops the awakening factors of mindfulness, investigation of principles, energy, rapture, tranquility, immersion, | Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu satisambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ virāganissitaṁ nirodhanissitaṁ vossaggapariṇāmiṁ …pe…
sn46.11:2.3 | and equanimity, which rely on seclusion, fading away, and cessation, and ripen as letting go. | upekkhāsambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ virāganissitaṁ nirodhanissitaṁ vossaggapariṇāmiṁ.
sn46.11:2.4 | That’s how a mendicant develops and cultivates the seven awakening factors depending on and grounded on ethics.” | Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sīlaṁ nissāya sīle patiṭṭhāya satta bojjhaṅge bhāveti, satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkarotī”ti.
