# sn46.29: SN 46.29 Ekadhammasutta — One Thing

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Nothing else, developed like this, leads to giving up the things that tighten the fetters like the seven factors (1.1, 2.3); the list (1.3); the stock formula for each factor (relies on seclusion, fading away and cessation; ripens as letting go) (2.2).

What we say we left out: What the things that tighten the fetters are: the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body and mind, where the fetters, shackles and attachments arise (3.1-3.8). Needs the six sense fields as a list and a pattern for 'X is where Y arises'.
