# sn46.26: SN 46.26 Taṇhakkhayasutta — The Ending of Craving

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The seven factors are the path that leads to the ending of craving (1.1, 1.4); the list (1.6); the stock formula for each factor (relies on seclusion, fading away and cessation; ripens as letting go) (2.1, 2.5).

What we say we left out: The chain: as they develop each factor, craving is given up; when craving is given up, deeds are given up; when deeds are given up, suffering is given up (2.2-2.4, 2.6-2.9). Needs a pattern for chains of ending, which dependent origination will also need. Also the description of each factor as 'abundant, expansive, limitless, and free of ill will' (2.1, 2.5).
