# sn46.48: SN 46.48 Ādiccasutta — The Sun

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Good friendship is the forerunner of the arising of the seven factors (1.2), and one with good friends can expect to develop them (1.3); the stock formula for each factor (relies on seclusion, fading away and cessation; ripens as letting go) (1.6). Same statements as SN 46.12, added as citations.

What we say we left out: The simile of the dawn (1.1).

sn46.48:0.2 | 5. A Wheel-Turning Monarch | 5. Cakkavattivagga
sn46.48:0.3 | The Sun | Ādiccasutta
sn46.48:1.1 | “Mendicants, the dawn is the forerunner and harbinger of the sunrise. | “Ādiccassa, bhikkhave, udayato etaṁ pubbaṅgamaṁ etaṁ pubbanimittaṁ, yadidaṁ—aruṇuggaṁ.
sn46.48:1.2 | In the same way, for a mendicant good friendship is the forerunner and harbinger of the arising of the seven awakening factors. | Evameva kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno sattannaṁ bojjhaṅgānaṁ uppādāya etaṁ pubbaṅgamaṁ etaṁ pubbanimittaṁ, yadidaṁ—kalyāṇamittatā.
sn46.48:1.3 | A mendicant with good friends can expect to develop and cultivate the seven awakening factors. | Kalyāṇamittassetaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno pāṭikaṅkhaṁ—satta bojjhaṅge bhāvessati, satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkarissati.
sn46.48:1.4 | And how does a mendicant with good friends develop and cultivate the seven awakening factors? | Kathañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhu kalyāṇamitto satta bojjhaṅge bhāveti, satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkaroti?
sn46.48:1.5 | It’s when a mendicant develops the awakening factors of mindfulness, investigation of principles, energy, rapture, tranquility, immersion, | Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu satisambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ …pe…
sn46.48:1.6 | and equanimity, which rely on seclusion, fading away, and cessation, and ripen as letting go. | upekkhāsambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ virāganissitaṁ nirodhanissitaṁ vossaggapariṇāmiṁ.
sn46.48:1.7 | That’s how a mendicant with good friends develops and cultivates the seven awakening factors.” | Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu kalyāṇamitto satta bojjhaṅge bhāveti, satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkarotī”ti.
