# dhp279: Dhammapada 279

Coverage status we recorded: **cited only**

What we say we encoded: The first line of the verse ('All things are not-self') as evidence for 'dhamma' meaning any phenomenon.

What we say we left out: The rest of the verse, and the rest of the Path chapter (Dhp 273-289).

## Claims citing this text

### sense-dhamma-phenomenon (word sense, layer: lexicon)
- summary: 'dhamma' meaning a dhamma (any phenomenon, any thing)
- segments cited in this text: dhp279:1
