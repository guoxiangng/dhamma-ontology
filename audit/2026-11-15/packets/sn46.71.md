# sn46.71: SN 46.71 Aniccasutta — Impermanence

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The one line given: the perception of impermanence (Pali: aniccasaññā), developed and cultivated, is very fruitful and beneficial (1.1).

What we say we left out: The rest of the sutta, which is abbreviated in the Pali (…pe…) and understood to repeat SN 46.57: developing it together with the awakening factors, and the further results. Not expanded here (D14).
