# sn46.28: SN 46.28 Nibbedhabhāgiyasutta — Helping Penetration

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The seven factors are the path that helps penetration (1.1, 1.5, 1.9, 2.9); the list (1.7); the stock formula for each factor (relies on seclusion, fading away and cessation; ripens as letting go) (2.1, 2.5).

What we say we left out: With a mind that has developed each factor, they penetrate and shatter the mass of greed, hate and delusion for the first time (2.2-2.4, 2.6-2.8); 'weakens' would understate 'shatter', so it waits for a better fit. Also 'abundant, expansive, limitless, and free of ill will' (2.1, 2.5).
