# mn44: MN 44 Cūḷavedallasutta — The Shorter Elaboration

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: One definition: 'Unification of the mind is immersion' (12.2). Found while checking an eval answer; it corrected decision D4, which had wrongly called this equation an interpretation.

What we say we left out: The rest of Dhammadinnā's answers: substantial reality, its origin, cessation and the practice leading there (2-5); grasping and the aggregates (6); substantialist view (7-8); the noble eightfold path and the three spectrums of practice (9-11); the bases, prerequisites and development of immersion (12.3-12.5); the three processes (13-15); the cessation of perception and feeling (16-21); the three feelings, their underlying tendencies and their counterparts (22-29).
