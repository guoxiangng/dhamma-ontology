# sn46.66: SN 46.66 Ānāpānasutta — Breathing

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The one line given: mindfulness of breathing (Pali: ānāpānassati), developed and cultivated, is very fruitful and beneficial (1.1).
