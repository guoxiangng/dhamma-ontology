# sn46.34: SN 46.34 Anupakkilesasutta — Not Corruptions

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: Developing the seven factors leads to the realization of the fruit of knowledge and freedom (1.1, 1.4); the list (1.3).

What we say we left out: That the factors are not obstacles, hindrances or corruptions of the mind (1.1, 1.4). Needs a pattern for what a quality is or is not.
