# sn46.56: SN 46.56 Abhayasutta — With Prince Abhaya

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The hindrances are a cause and reason for not knowing and not seeing, when the heart is overcome by them (2.3-3.5); the awakening factors are a cause and reason for knowing and seeing (5.3-6.4); the stock formula (5.3, 6.2).

What we say we left out: The framing: Pūraṇa Kassapa's view that knowing and not knowing have no cause (1.4-1.13). The naming 'these are called the hindrances / awakening factors' (4.2, 7.2). Prince Abhaya's responses, including 'overcome by even a single hindrance you wouldn't truly know or see' and 'endowed with even a single awakening factor you would' (4.3-4.5, 7.3-7.6): said by the interlocutor, not taught, so not encoded (D17).
