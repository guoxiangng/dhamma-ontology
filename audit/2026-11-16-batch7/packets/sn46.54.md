# sn46.54: SN 46.54 Mettāsahagatasutta — Full of Love

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: How each heart's release (by love, compassion, rejoicing, equanimity) is developed: each awakening factor developed accompanied by that quality, with the stock formula (12.1-12.3, 13.1-13.3, 14.1-14.3, 15.1-15.3; the Pali's direction, not the English's, D21). The apex of each, for a wise mendicant who has not penetrated to a higher freedom: the beautiful; the dimension of infinite space; of infinite consciousness; of nothingness (12.8-12.9, 13.6-13.7, 14.6-14.7, 15.9-15.10).

What we say we left out: The narrative frame and the wanderers' challenge (1.1-10.2), including their restatement of the teaching: give up the five hindrances, corruptions of the heart that weaken wisdom, and spread a heart full of love, compassion, rejoicing and equanimity in every direction (3.2-3.5, 4.2-4.6, 8.2-9.5): said by the interlocutors (D17). The Buddha's counter-questions and 'they're out of their element' (11.1-11.9). The five ways of perceiving at will: the repulsive in the unrepulsive, the unrepulsive in the repulsive, and so on, and staying equanimous (12.4-12.8, 13.4-13.5, 14.4-14.5, 15.4-15.8): no pattern yet. The questions also ask each release's destination, fruit and end; only the apex is answered.
