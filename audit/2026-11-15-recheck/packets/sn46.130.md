# sn46.130: SN 46.130 Uddhambhāgiyasutta — Higher Fetters

Coverage status we recorded: **fully encoded**

What we say we encoded: The five higher fetters as a list (1.2, 1.4); the seven factors should be developed for their direct knowledge, complete understanding, finishing and giving up (1.6, 1.12); and the formula variants: culminating in the removal of greed, hate and delusion (1.9), having freedom from death as their culmination (1.10), and inclining to extinguishment (1.11); and the seclusion formula at 1.8, which is in the Pali but not the English of that segment (added 2026-11-15, D19). The rest of the sutta is abbreviated in the source.

What we say we left out: (nothing)
