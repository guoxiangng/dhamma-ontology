# sn46.3: SN 46.3 Sīlasutta — Ethics

Coverage status we recorded: **partly encoded**

What we say we encoded: The awakening factors arising in sequence, each step a condition with its own words: recollecting and thinking about the teaching gives rise to mindfulness, then investigation, energy, rapture (not of the flesh), tranquility, immersion (with bliss) and equanimity (1.9-8.1); and the seven fruits and benefits they lead to, from enlightenment early in this life to heading upstream to the Akaniṭṭha realm (9.1-9.10).

What we say we left out: Why it matters to see, hear, approach, pay homage to, recollect and go forth following mendicants accomplished in ethics, immersion, knowledge, freedom, and the knowledge and vision of freedom: 'very helpful' (1.1-1.6); and living withdrawn in body and mind after hearing their teaching (1.8). Needs a pattern for what is called helpful, and the link from hearing to withdrawal is not modelled. Within the sequence, each factor is 'initiated', 'developed' and 'perfected' (e.g. 2.2-2.3); only its arising is modelled. Fruits 3-7 come 'with the ending of the five lower fetters' (9.5-9.9): kept in a note, not modelled.
