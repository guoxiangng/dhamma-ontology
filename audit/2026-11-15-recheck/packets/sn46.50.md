# sn46.50: SN 46.50 Bāhiraṅgasutta — Exterior

Coverage status we recorded: **fully encoded**

What we say we encoded: Of exterior factors, nothing gives rise to the seven awakening factors like good friendship (1.1); one with good friends can expect to develop and cultivate them (1.2), by the stock formula (1.3-1.6). Corrected 2026-11-15: 1.2-1.6 had been called abbreviated; they are untranslated in the English but given in the Pali (D19). The chapter's summary verse (2.0-2.4) is not a teaching.

What we say we left out: (nothing)
