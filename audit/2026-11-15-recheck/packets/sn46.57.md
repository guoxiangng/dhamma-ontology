# sn46.57: SN 46.57 Aṭṭhikamahapphalasutta — A Skeleton

Coverage status we recorded: **fully encoded**

What we say we encoded: The perception of a skeleton is very fruitful and beneficial (1.2, 1.6), developed together with the awakening factors (1.4), with the stock formula (1.5); one of two results, enlightenment in this very life or non-return; great benefit, great sanctuary from the yoke, great inspiration and great ease.

What we say we left out: (nothing)
