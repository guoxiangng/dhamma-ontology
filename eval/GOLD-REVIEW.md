## Q18 · trap · scope: covered · status: draft

**Q:** Does SN 46.51 say that the awakening factor of tranquility counters restlessness and remorse?

**Gold:**
- No. SN 46.51 says restlessness and remorse is starved by rational attention to the settled mind; tranquility is fuelled by rational attention to tranquility of body and mind. Different objects; the sutta doesn't link them.
- A related but different teaching is SN 46.53: when the mind is restless, it is the right time to develop tranquility, immersion and equanimity.

**Text:**
- [`sn46.51:16.2`](https://suttacentral.net/sn46.51/en/sujato#sn46.51:16.2) There is the settled mind.  
  _Atthi, bhikkhave, cetaso vūpasamo._
- [`sn46.51:16.3`](https://suttacentral.net/sn46.51/en/sujato#sn46.51:16.3) Frequent rational application of mind to that

## Q38 · trap · scope: covered · status: draft

**Q:** Does SN 46.1 say that ethics is the forerunner of the awakening factors?

**Gold:**
- No. SN 46.1 says the seven awakening factors are developed depending on and grounded on ethics (sīla): ethics as their basis.
- 'Forerunner' is SN 46.12's and SN 46.13's word, for good friendship and rational application of mind.

**Text:**
- [`sn46.1:1.4`](https://suttacentral.net/sn46.1/en/sujato#sn46.1:1.4) In the same way, a mendicant develops and cultivates the seven awakening factors depending on and grounded on ethics, acquiring great and abundant good qualities.  
  _evameva kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sīlaṁ nissāya sīle patiṭṭhāya satta bojjhaṅge bhāvento satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkaronto mahantattaṁ vepullattaṁ pāpuṇāti dhammesu._
- [`sn46.12:1.2`](https://suttacentral.net/sn46.12/en/sujato#sn46.12:1.2) In the same way, for a mendicant good friendship is the forerunner and harbinger of the arising of the seven awakening factors.  
  _evameva kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno sattannaṁ bojjhaṅgānaṁ uppādāya etaṁ pubbaṅgamaṁ etaṁ nimittaṁ, yadidaṁ—kalyāṇamittatā._
- [`sn46.13:1.2`](https://suttacentral.net/sn46.13/en/sujato#sn46.13:1.2) In the same way, for a mendicant rational application of mind is the forerunner and harbinger of the arising of the seven awakening factors.  
  _evameva kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno sattannaṁ bojjhaṅgānaṁ uppādāya etaṁ pubbaṅgamaṁ etaṁ pubbanimittaṁ, yadidaṁ—yonisomanasikāro._
