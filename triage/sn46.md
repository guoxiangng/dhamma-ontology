## Waiting for a pattern

### A. "Developing X leads to Y" (and what a quality does)

This is the most common shape in SN 46: the awakening factors, developed and cultivated, lead to an outcome. Some suttas also describe what the hindrances do, e.g. weaken wisdom.

SN 46.5, 46.7, 46.17, 46.18, 46.19, 46.20, 46.21, 46.25, 46.26, 46.27, 46.28, 46.29, 46.34, 46.36, 46.41, 46.43, 46.130. Also the perception series SN 46.57–76: a meditation subject developed together with the awakening factors is very fruitful (46.57 and 46.76 in full, the rest abbreviated). Also the abbreviated repetition series SN 46.77–184, which point back to SN 45.

### B. Conditions: "X is the forerunner, root or basis of Y" / "when X, then Y"

SN 46.1, 46.3, 46.11, 46.12, 46.13, 46.24, 46.31, 46.32, 46.35, 46.48, 46.49, 46.50, 46.56. Also SN 46.38 (attentive listening → hindrances absent).

The same pattern is needed later for dependent origination (SN 12), so agreeing on it here pays twice.

### C. Similes

SN 46.1, 46.7, 46.11, 46.12, 46.13, 46.39, 46.42, 46.48, 46.55, 46.77–88, plus the similes already left out of SN 46.2, 46.33 and 46.53.

### D. Other shapes, one or two texts each

- **Person types defined by the factors:** SN 46.44–47 ("witless", "wise", "poor", "prosperous"). Probably an extension of the definition pattern.
- **Subdivisions:** SN 46.52, where the five hindrances become ten and the seven factors fourteen. Central to this topic, so worth doing early.
- **Narrative and practice accounts**, still to be assessed in full: SN 46.4, 46.6, 46.8, 46.14–16, 46.30, 46.54.

## Suggested order

1. **Agree patterns A and B.** Together they unlock about 30 suttas here, and they're needed again for the 37 wings of awakening and for dependent origination.
2. **SN 46.52** (subdivisions), because it deepens the core topic.
3. **Similes (C).**
