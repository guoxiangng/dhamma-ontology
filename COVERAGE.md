This is how much of the canon the ontology actually draws on. It is small on purpose: a text only counts once its claims are modelled and cited, and partial work says what it leaves out.

**Who did the modelling.** The project owner is a practitioner, not a teacher, and the modelling was drafted with an AI assistant (Claude). The claims are the texts' own words, quoted from SuttaCentral; the *structure* (what counts as a claim, how it is split, which editor links exist) is our interpretation. Until a text is marked reviewed, treat its modelling as a draft.
